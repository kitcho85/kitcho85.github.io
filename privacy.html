Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Save The Sleeping Turtle ("the Game", "we", "us", or "our") handles information when you download and play the Game.

We respect your privacy and are committed to being transparent about the third-party services used by the Game.

Information We Collect

The Game does not directly collect information such as your name, home address, phone number, or other information that you directly provide to us.

However, the Game uses third-party services such as Google AdMob, Firebase Analytics, and Google Play Games Services. These services may collect or process certain technical, usage, advertising, or account-related information according to their own privacy policies.

Firebase Analytics

The Game uses Google Firebase Analytics to understand how players use the Game and to improve gameplay and the overall user experience.

The Game may send anonymous or pseudonymous gameplay events, including information such as:

  • Game starts
  • Level starts and completions
  • Level failures and retries
  • Stars earned
  • Gameplay time and line length
  • Hints used
  • Rewarded advertisements
  • Shop activity
  • Turtle skin selections
  • Leaf currency earned and spent
  • Tutorial progress
  • Daily rewards
  • Rate popup interactions

Firebase Analytics may also process technical information associated with the device or application, as described in Google's policies and documentation.

We use this information to understand game performance, identify problems, improve gameplay, and make the Game better.

Advertising

The Game uses Google AdMob to display advertisements.

Google AdMob may collect and process information such as device information, advertising identifiers, IP address, approximate location, app interactions, diagnostics, and other information required to provide, measure, and improve advertising services, prevent fraud, and comply with applicable policies.

Advertising data may be processed by Google according to Google's Privacy Policy and applicable advertising policies.

You can learn more about how Google uses information for advertising through Google's privacy resources.

Google Play Games Services

The Game may use Google Play Games Services to provide features such as player authentication, achievements, leaderboards, and player information.

If you choose to sign in with Google Play Games, the Game may receive information provided by Google Play Games, such as your display name, player identifier, and profile image.

This information is used only to provide Google Play Games features within the Game.

Google processes Google Play Games information according to its own privacy policies and terms.

Achievements and Leaderboards

The Game may submit achievement progress and leaderboard scores to Google Play Games Services when you are signed in.

These features are provided through Google's gaming services and are subject to Google's privacy policy and terms of service.

Game Progress and Local Storage

The Game stores certain game progress and settings locally on your device.

This may include information such as unlocked levels, earned stars, selected turtle skins, leaf currency, game settings, and other gameplay progress.

This information is stored locally using Unity PlayerPrefs and is not directly uploaded by the Game to our own servers.

In-App Purchases

The Game may offer optional in-app purchases through Google Play Billing.

If in-app purchases are available, payment transactions are processed securely by Google Play.

We do not receive or store your credit card number, bank account information, or other payment credentials.

Google Play may process purchase-related information according to Google's privacy policy and applicable terms.

Children's Privacy

Save The Sleeping Turtle is intended for a general audience.

We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

The third-party services used by the Game, including Google AdMob, Firebase Analytics, and Google Play Games Services, operate under their own policies and age-related requirements.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the Game, please contact us so that we can review the situation.

Third-Party Services

The Game uses the following third-party services:

  • Google AdMob
  • Google Firebase Analytics
  • Google Play Games Services
  • Google Play Billing, when applicable

These services may collect or process information according to their own privacy policies.

Data Security

We take reasonable steps to avoid collecting unnecessary personal information directly through the Game.

Information processed by third-party services is handled according to the security practices and policies of those service providers.

Data Retention

Game progress stored locally remains on your device until it is deleted by the Game, cleared through your device settings, or the Game is uninstalled.

Information processed by third-party services is retained and handled according to the policies of those service providers.

Changes to This Privacy Policy
